# Habit_tracker

I built a daily habit tracker , and it has a tiny pet named Blip living inside it. Here is everything the app does and why a good habit tracker is worth having.

What is inside:
- 10 daily habits with dot-matrix progress bars
- Day, week, month, quarter and year views
- Streaks, a trend chart, a habit breakdown, a best-days chart and an 18-week heatmap
- A to-do list, a quote card, and light and dark themes
- Blip, a pet that follows your cursor, hops around, dances and sleeps in its own house
